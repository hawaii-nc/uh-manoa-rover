How to go through the map

Fly F: drag to turn, right-drag to move, scroll to zoom. Double-click the ground to stand there.

Walk P (person, eye 1.7 m) and Rover R (camera 0.7 m): WASD or arrow keys to move, drag to look, Shift to go faster. You cannot walk through buildings. The rover cannot climb a step higher than 12 cm, so curbs and stairs stop it (the status bar says why).

N add a note here · C show/hide check markers · T cycle the look · G cycle the network · [ ] previous / next check · ? this help.

Click any surface to see what the model says it is. Pick a check in the list, go there, and mark it Looks right, Looks wrong or Unsure with a note. Copy my notes puts everything on the clipboard so you can paste it back. Your marks stay in this browser.

This is a model built from open data. Everything it shows as measured comes with a source; anything assumed is listed under Legend & data.
